4×4 (Four-Wheel Drive) sends power to all four wheels and is usually designed for off-road use with selectable modes. AWD (All-Wheel Drive) automatically distributes power without driver input, ideal for city/highway + light off-roading. 4×2 drives only two wheels (front or rear), offering better fuel efficiency but less traction on rough terrain.
